SCRIPT PACE PRIVACY

Your words stay yours.

Effective October 22, 2026

Script Pace is developed by Clayton Schubiner. This policy describes the Script Pace iPhone and iPad app and this support website.

Data collection

Script Pace does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data, scripts, or camera images to the developer or an app backend. The app contains no advertising, tracking, analytics, third-party SDKs, or account system.

Scripts and files

Scripts you create or import are stored in the app’s local library. When you explicitly import a file, Script Pace reads the selected file and saves a local script. When you export or share text, you choose the destination; that destination’s privacy terms apply. Deleting the app removes its local library. Device-level backup and restore may be managed by your operating system settings.

Camera access

Camera access is optional and requested only after you tap Enable camera. It provides a live preview and, on supported multi-display configurations, enables a synchronized outer-display reader. Script Pace does not record video or audio, request microphone access, save camera images, or upload the preview. You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings.

Timing

The app uses system timing solely to measure elapsed time during rehearsal and advance your script at the selected pace. This information stays in the app and is not used to identify you or your device.

Purchases and this website

Apple handles App Store payments and downloads under its own privacy policy. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP addresses. The site adds no analytics or tracking cookies.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Do not include private script content unless you intend to share it. Contact clayschubiner@gmail.com for privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
