SCRIPT PACE SUPPORT

Ready for another take.

Need a hand? Email clayschubiner@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, and what happened. Please avoid sending private scripts or camera images unless you intend to share them.

Getting started

  1. Tap + to create a script, enter a title and text, then Save. Or import a UTF-8 .txt file smaller than 2 MB.
  2. Tap Rehearse. Adjust pace, press Play, and use Pause or Restart whenever you need.
  3. Open Reader settings to adjust text size, horizontal mirroring, upside-down reading, and the countdown. Settings apply to the current rehearsal.

Optional outer-display reader

This feature requires a compatible multi-display device with iOS 27.1 or later, the app full screen, and an active camera session. Tap Enable camera and grant camera access if you want the live preview. The system controls accessory availability. Use the outer-reader toggle when it is available. No video or audio is recorded.

Where are my scripts?

Scripts are stored in this app’s local library. Use a script’s menu to export a copy for safekeeping. Deleting the app removes its library. Your device’s system backup settings may also apply. There is no Script Pace cloud-sync service.

Storage or import errors

Choose a UTF-8 plain text file under 2 MB. If saving fails, keep the app open and export your work. A damaged or unrecognized library is preserved rather than automatically overwritten; contact support for recovery help.

Purchases

Script Pace is a one-time paid app with no subscription or in-app purchases. Apple handles payment, downloads, purchase history, and refund requests through your App Store account.
